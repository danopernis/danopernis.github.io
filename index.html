Climbing is fun!

I fell in love with climbing during the COVID pandemic. Being outside with friends, breathing fresh air, and enjoying something which I thought was reserved only for the elite left me wanting to explore more. I later joined the mountaineering club HO Humanita. I sincerely recommend them to anybody from Prague who is interested in climbing and wants friendly company for safe outdoor adventures.

Kung-fu is fun!

I have been practicing kung-fu with sifu Pavel Macek for more than a decade now, and I can't recommend it enough. Learning some basic self-defence and fighting is useful on its own, but kung-fu teaches you to overcome obstacles, strengthen your body, improve your flexibility and posture, calm your mind, and much, much more!
